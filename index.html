Common pattern in open feedback
Virtual sessions rated lower than in-person. Fellows who had colleagues in the cohort (AmaliTech team) found extra value from shared reflection.
Key recommendations — prioritized by frequency
#1 Most urgentSend all session materials 3–5 days before. Fellows consistently mentioned last-minute delivery as a barrier to preparation.
#2Add a structured peer connection moment mid-cohort — a facilitated virtual coffee or dedicated session. Do not leave networking entirely self-directed.
#3Reinstate pre/post work accountability. Ask fellows to submit short snippets so the work feels "real" and is not easily skipped.
#4Share clear session agendas in advance. Fellows wanted to prepare specific questions but didn't know the session focus until they arrived.
#5Introduce the advisor network earlier and pair it with structured 1:1 meetings. Half-way introduction led to low uptake.
#6 NYU specificBuild more reflection and processing time into each day. Consider extending to 1.5–2 weeks to reduce intensity. Share exercise instructions before breakout groups begin.
#7 NYU specificReview the 180-day growth plan with fellows in coaching sessions. Fellows invested in this tool but felt no one checked it or gave feedback on it.
#8Consider splitting cohort content by company stage. Some topics (early startup) were not relevant to fellows at larger or more mature companies.
